# Twitch

The Streams section shows live streams and videos of Twitch channels. Kinolune is not affiliated with Twitch; channels, videos and streams come from Twitch.

## Turning it on

Settings → Twitch streams → **Show Twitch**. Streams then appears in the menu. For kid profiles and profiles with restrictions it is hidden by default.

## Without and with signing in

Without signing in you can add channels to Favorites by name: the Search tab → type the name → "Add channel <name>".

Sign in to see who is live, new videos and followed channels, and to write in chat:

1. "Sign in with Twitch".
2. On your phone, open `twitch.tv/activate` (or scan the QR code) and enter the code shown.
3. The window closes by itself. Kinolune never sees your Twitch password; it keeps only an access key.

To sign out: tap your account in the header → "Sign out of Twitch".

## What is in the section

- **Home**: Live now, Favorites (channels can be pinned to the top), Continue Watching, New videos, Soon live, Categories of your channels.
- **Channels**: favorites and followed channels, a Live / Offline filter, sorting.
- **Search**: channels and categories.
- **Channel page**: "Watch live", favorite, live alerts on/off, "Open chat", tabs Videos, Highlights, Clips, Schedule, About.

Subscriber-only videos and clips open on the Twitch website: Kinolune shows a link and a QR code.

## Player

- **GitHub (full) builds** play streams and videos in Kinolune's own player: low latency, "Go live", "Quality" and "Audio only", chapters and speed for videos, chat beside or over the video (theatre mode). Twitch ads play as they are and are not blocked.
- **Store builds** (once they exist) use the official Twitch player, or an "Open on Twitch" link where it is not available.

If the player fails to start, there are "Try again" and "Open in the Twitch player".

## Chat

Chat can be read without signing in. To write, sign in (with chat permission). Chat settings let you hide bots, highlight your name and chosen words, block users and add word filters. "Pause animated emotes" is in the chat header and in Settings → Twitch streams. On a TV, chat is read-only.

## Live alerts

When signed in, turn on live alerts on the page of a favourite channel to get a system notification "<channel> is live". On Android the app asks for notification permission; with the app closed, checks run in the background at most every 15 minutes. Windows has no such notifications yet.

## Downloads and recording (full builds only)

- **Download** in a video's player — the whole video or one chapter. The list is under Downloads.
- **Recording** on a channel page — an automatic recording rule: quality, "Record chat", how many streams or days to keep, categories. The first time, Kinolune reminds you that recordings are for personal use only: Twitch's terms do not allow redistributing or re-uploading them.
- **Recordings and downloads** — saved files; "Import" adds files downloaded with other tools.
- Settings → Twitch streams → Recordings and downloads — folders, a storage quota and file name templates.

Recording runs while Kinolune is open. Kid profiles do not see recordings and downloads.
